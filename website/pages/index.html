Pretorsion theories and image-closed subcategories

The intersections of the two components of pretorsion theories are exactly the image-closed subcategories, and the pretorsion theories form a complete lattice, checked in Lean.

Let Λ be a finite-dimensional algebra over a field k. A pretorsion theory (𝒯, ℱ) in mod Λ generalizes a torsion pair: morphisms from 𝒯 to ℱ factor through 𝒵 = 𝒯 ∩ ℱ, and every module sits in a sequence 𝒯 ∋ T → X → F ∈ ℱ that is exact up to morphisms factoring through 𝒵. A subcategory is image-closed if it contains the image of every morphism between its objects.

Theorem A. For a subcategory 𝒵 of mod Λ, the following are equivalent: 𝒵 is image-closed; 𝒵 = Gen 𝒵 ∩ Cogen 𝒵; 𝒵 = 𝒯 ∩ ℱ for a pretorsion theory (𝒯, ℱ).
Theorem 3.6. The pretorsion theories of mod Λ, ordered by (𝒯, ℱ) ≤ (𝒯′, ℱ′) iff 𝒯 ⊆ 𝒯′ and ℱ ⊇ ℱ′, form a complete lattice.
Theorem B. For the linearly oriented quiver 1 → 2 → ⋯ → n, counting pretorsion theories by the number of indecomposables in 𝒯 ∩ ℱ gives Bn+1(1, q), and counting image-closed subcategories by their number of indecomposables gives Cn+1(1, q), the q-Gandhi polynomials of Han and Zeng.

Verification and provenance

The paper

The paper Pretorsion theories and image-closed subcategories by Haruhisa Enomoto characterizes the intersections of pretorsion theories, describes the lattice of pretorsion theories and the realizations of an image-closed subcategory, studies image closure as a convex geometry on indecomposable modules, and counts pretorsion theories and image-closed subcategories for a linearly oriented quiver of type A. This repository proves every numbered statement of the paper and the cited inputs it uses. The correspondence record maps each numbered statement to its Lean declarations.
