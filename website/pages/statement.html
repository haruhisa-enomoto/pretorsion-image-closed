The formal theorems

Modules are finite-dimensional right modules over a finite-dimensional algebra Λ, formalized as finitely generated modules over Λop. A subcategory is a property of objects closed under isomorphisms, finite direct sums and direct summands. A morphism is 𝒵-trivial if it factors through an object of 𝒵; prekernels, precokernels and pretorsion theories follow Definition 2.2 of the paper. The category mod Λn of the linear quiver is the category of functors from the linear order Fin n to finite-dimensional vector spaces. The number of indecomposables of a subcategory is the number of isomorphism classes of its indecomposable objects. The q-Gandhi polynomials live in ℤ[q][x], and g = Δqf is expressed by g · (1 + (q − 1)x) = f(1 + qx) − f(x). All types live in one universe.
